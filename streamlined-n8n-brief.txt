Build “Nous UK Creator Discovery and Collaboration Emails” in n8n Cloud.

PRIMARY OUTPUT
A reviewable shortlist of qualified Instagram creators, their publicly listed business emails, and personalised collaboration EMAIL drafts, with Instagram message drafts when no usable email is available. Store everything in n8n Data Tables. Do not send anything or create Gmail drafts.

BUDGET AND DEFAULTS
£0 additional spend. Use SerpApi's free plan, existing n8n allowance, native nodes and rule-based Code-node templates. No paid AI APIs, Apify, Google Sheets or external databases.
Manual trigger. Editable defaults: UK; 10,000–100,000 followers inclusive; 5 profiles per test run; sample target 20 unique posts; 5 substantive relevant posts required; 15 total SerpApi search attempts per run; blank sender name/role; approved brief “Nous helps UK households manage insurance admin.”; optional confirmed product facts.
Keep the key in n8n credentials. Never save keys, raw account responses or credential-bearing URLs.
Check Account API for an active zero-price plan and sufficient free monthly/hourly allowance before collection. Recheck before search attempts. Share the 15-attempt cap across searches, profile requests, pagination, contact searches and retries. Count failed attempts. Track free Account API checks separately. Use finite timeouts and at most one transient retry. Stop/defer on quota exhaustion. If this account is also used by a fallback, avoid simultaneous collection runs.

One Google page for each query, with UK/English settings:

site:instagram.com "UK" "money saving"
site:instagram.com "UK" "household bills"

Immediately upsert normalised search results BEFORE enrichment. Deduplicate by query + canonical URL. Extract handles only from unambiguous profile URLs; retain unresolved post links for review. Exclude reserved Instagram paths.

2. QUALIFY WITH EVIDENCE
Use engine=instagram_profile and profile_id=username. Read profile_results: identity, biography, numeric followers, bio_links and posts[].media_captions. Keep saved evidence links. For in-range profiles, collect up to 20 unique posts with at most one additional page using serpapi_pagination.next_page_token.
Screen for insurance/renewals, family budgeting/saving, household bills/contracts, home ownership, subscriptions, document organisation/life admin and parenting mental load.
Keywords identify candidates for review, not substantive relevance. Qualification requires the follower range, at least five relevant posts among the latest 20, and supported UK-household suitability. Do not call the sample “latest 20” without evidence. Missing criteria go to review. Support an explicit manual approval with reviewer notes and preserve it during reruns. Keep manual approval distinct from evidence-qualified status. Do not infer audience geography from creator location.

3. FIND A BUSINESS EMAIL
Research qualified/manually approved creators AND promising in-range candidates with saved relevant biography or caption evidence, even while final qualification is pending. Explicitly rejected profiles stay excluded. Missing follower counts or out-of-range followers require review/manual approval. Keep qualification status unchanged during contact research. Start with a suitable saved email and then the biography. STOP additional collection once a suitably attributed email exists.
If unresolved, fetch publicly accessible bio links and clearly linked Contact / Work With Me / Management pages. Maximum FOUR public-page requests per creator, including redirects; stop early when successful. Collect business and representative emails with exact supporting text and source URL. Identify creator versus representative. Ignore privacy, support and vendor addresses. Do not invent names or email addresses.
If still unresolved, permit ONE targeted SerpApi search using the handle and business enquiries / collaborations / management within the shared cap. Search-snippet-only evidence stays unconfirmed. Keep discovered, human-confirmed, approved and rejected contact states distinct. Syntax does not establish ownership or deliverability. Record found / no email / retrieval failed / deferred by quota.

4. DRAFT A COLLABORATION EMAIL
Read saved biography and post evidence. Use an exact, relevant reference and topic-specific rule-based wording. Budgeting, insurance and household-organisation creators should get distinct openings and potential audience-benefit framing.
Use only the approved product brief/facts for Nous claims. Never invent savings, coverage, pricing, partnerships, credentials, audience demographics or claims to have watched unavailable videos.
For each suitable attributable business email belonging to an eligible research candidate, save a concise subject and approximately 100–150-word email: supported personalisation, potential relevance to followers, a possible paid partnership without promised fees/terms, and a question about discussing collaboration. Address management appropriately and name the creator. Use sender placeholders until supplied. Set needs_review and retain evidence links, product facts used and missing-information flags. If creator qualification is pending, visibly mark the draft provisional and include qualification_pending in exports. Never treat finding an email as approving the creator.
If lookup finds no usable email, fails, reaches quota, or leaves attribution unconfirmed, automatically create a 50–80-word Instagram message draft. Reference saved biography/caption evidence, use only approved product claims, describe potential relevance, and ask whether they are open to a paid partnership and which business email to use. Save channel=instagram_message, no invented email, and needs_review. Label pending qualification and the actual lookup reason. Do not claim no email exists after a failed lookup. Never send the message. Preserve edited messages; if a usable email later appears, retain the message as history and make the email the current outreach option. Never overwrite manually edited or approved drafts.
